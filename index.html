About The Foundation

Advancing Cambodia’s Bio-Economy through rigorous empirical rigor.

The Research Foundation is dedicated to fostering regional innovation by applying molecular biotechnology, biochemical valorization, and reproducible statistical frameworks to real-world agrarian challenges along the Mekong basin.

Operating primary initiatives in Kratié Province in tandem with national academic collaborations in Phnom Penh, our team develops both tangible laboratory solutions and statistical analytical software accessible to scientists globally.

Applied Research

Direct laboratory and localized agrarian field experiments.

Statistical Computing

Transparent algorithms tailored for high-variance biological datasets.

Microbial Biotechnology

v2.4 Open Release

Research Foundation Analyzer

A streamlined, accessible statistical computing program tailored for agricultural researchers, agronomists, and biotechnology laboratories. Run split-plot designs, Factorial RCBD, Duncan/Tukey post-hoc tests, and regression models without cumbersome commercial licensing.

Complete experimental design support: CRD, RCBD, Latin Square, and Split-Plot.
Automated generation of publication-standard ANOVA summary tables.
Runs natively in modern web browsers and offline environments.
